# Casey Centres Atlas

The public edition of the Atlas for the review of the City of Casey's Activity Centres Strategy (2020): the findings,
walk access, the map, what the community has said in published consultations, and every public source behind them.

**Live site:** https://yashcccstrat.github.io/ATLAS/

This repository holds only the built website. It is generated automatically from a private working repository and
replaced on every update, so changes made here are overwritten.

- **Not Council policy.** A working analysis supporting the review. It is one input to officer analysis, not a
  decision or a ranking.
- **AI-assisted.** Prepared with generative AI (Claude, Anthropic) under the City of Casey Generative AI Policy (2025),
  using only public information. Figures trace to published sources; the engagement coding awaits an officer check.
- **Left out of this public edition:** the AI's paraphrased engagement findings, .id forecast figures and stored files.
- **Data:** City of Casey open data (CC BY 3.0); ABS Census 2021 (suburbs, SA1 areas and mesh blocks) and SEIFA 2021
  (CC BY 4.0); Department of Transport and Planning: Victoria in Future 2023, Urban Development Program 2017, 2018,
  2022 and 2025, and Plan for Victoria housing targets (CC BY 4.0); Transport Victoria GTFS (CC BY 4.0); Overture
  Maps; basemap © CARTO and © OpenStreetMap contributors. Documents are linked to their publishers' own copies, not
  stored here.
- **What changed and when:** see [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md), or the "What's new" section on the site.
